ABOUT FUSION CLUB

IOT, ROBOTICS & DRONES CLUB · GLA UNIVERSITY

Where Ideas Get Built

FUSION Club is the IoT, Robotics and Drones Club of GLA University — a student community that builds with sensors, circuits, robots, drones and code, and turns curiosity into working prototypes.

FUSION Club is the club organising the entire Tech-A-Week 1.0 — the Ideathon, Tech Debate, Project Exhibition and both workshops — in collaboration with the AI, Cloud and Cyber clubs and Meraki Chronicles.

EVENT

Ideathon

Organising Clubs

Collaboration between AI, Cloud, Cyber and FUSION (IoT, Robotics & Drones) Clubs.

Objective
Early-stage innovation and problem pitching focusing on tech-driven solutions to real-world challenges.
Team Structure
2–4 students per team.
Pitch Format
8 minutes total per team: a 5-minute presentation (maximum 7-slide PPT outlining the problem, target audience, tech architecture and proposed solution) followed by a 3-minute Q&A with the jury.
Audience Interaction
Presentations are conducted openly in front of all participants, and the audience can ask questions to the presenting team.

Registration opening soon

EVENT

Tech Debate

Organising Clubs

Collaboration between FUSION (IoT, Robotics & Drones) Club and Meraki Chronicles (Literature Club).

Objective
A technical debate for B.Tech 1st Year (AIML, CSE, ECE, AI) on contemporary AI and tech topics.
Format & Teams
4 students per team. Matches are 10–12 minutes long, consisting of opening statements, a rebuttal round and judges' Q&A.
Flow
Round 1 (Screening): 10:30 AM – 1:00 PM.
Final Round: 2:40 PM – 4:40 PM.
Debate Topics
Not decided yet. Every club will have different topics — revealing soon.

Registration opening soon

EVENT

Project Exhibition

Organising Club

Completely organised by the FUSION Club (IoT, Robotics & Drones).

Objective
A physical tech showcase where students can exhibit their innovative hardware projects.
Focus Area
Hands-on prototypes and functional tech models. Innovative hardware projects only.
